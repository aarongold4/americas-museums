Atlanta's museum scene is concentrated in three areas. Downtown, the Pemberton Place complex puts the National Center for Civil and Human Rights, World of Coca-Cola, and the Children's Museum next to the Georgia Aquarium on a single plaza. Midtown holds the High Museum, the Center for Puppetry Arts, and the Museum of Design Atlanta. Buckhead and the eastern neighborhoods hold the Atlanta History Center, Fernbank, and the Carter Library.

The National Center for Civil and Human Rights is the museum most distinctive to Atlanta — its lunch-counter sit-in simulator is genuinely demanding and unmatched as a physical-empathy exhibit. The Center for Puppetry Arts holds the world's largest Jim Henson collection, donated by Henson personally.
