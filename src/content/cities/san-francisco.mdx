San Francisco's museums divide between two main concentrations. Golden Gate Park holds the de Young, the California Academy of Sciences, and the Asian Art Museum's planning office (the museum itself is in Civic Center). The downtown core — SoMa, Yerba Buena, and the Embarcadero — holds SFMOMA, the Contemporary Jewish Museum, the Museum of the African Diaspora, and the Exploratorium.

Bay Area residents get free first-Sundays at most major museums, including the de Young, Legion of Honor, and Asian Art Museum. The Cable Car Museum and the GLBT Historical Society Museum are always free.

The Exploratorium's After Dark on Thursdays (21+) is worth a separate evening — it's the city's best after-hours museum experience.
