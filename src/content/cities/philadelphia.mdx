Philadelphia's museums cluster on two axes. The Benjamin Franklin Parkway — Philadelphia's Champs-Élysées — runs from City Hall to the Art Museum and lines up the Franklin Institute, the Barnes Foundation, the Rodin Museum, and the Philadelphia Museum of Art on a single one-mile walk. The Independence Mall area — Old City and the Constitution Center — houses the National Constitution Center, the Weitzman National Museum of American Jewish History, and the African American Museum.

Two specialist institutions are reasons to visit Philadelphia on their own. The Mütter Museum's medical specimens are unmatched anywhere; Eastern State Penitentiary is the country's best example of preserved 19th-century carceral architecture, and its Halloween Nights program runs October–November.
