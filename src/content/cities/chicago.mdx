Chicago is one of the few American cities where you can fairly call the museum scene world-class. Three of the country's most important institutions — the Art Institute, the Field, and the Museum of Science and Industry — are within walking distance of each other on or near the lakefront, and a half-dozen smaller museums fill in the gaps with depth that most cities can't match.

Most are paid, but several have always-free admission, and almost all offer free or discounted days for Illinois residents. The list below is ranked by how confidently I'd send a first-time visitor. The full directory of every Chicago museum (40+) is at the bottom.

If you're doing the lakefront trio in one day, start with the Field at opening, walk twenty minutes south for Adler around lunch, and end with MSI in Hyde Park if you have stamina (or skip it if you don't — it deserves its own day).
